# Naya Kai — Brand Guideline
The visual system is proposed, not finalised.

## 1. The Name

| Element | Root and meaning | Brand interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Naya** | Associated with method, way, plan, policy, reasoning, guidance and sound judgement | **The considered path.** Structured thinking that frames the problem, follows evidence and arrives at a practical outcome. |
| **Kai** | A compact Japanese-rooted element that can evoke meeting, association, group, connection or practice | **The practice and the people.** Collaboration, craft and the repeated work of turning ideas into useful systems. |

**Pronunciation:** `NAY-ah KYE`

Naya Kai is an intentional cross-cultural pairing, not a literal bilingual phrase. **Naya** comes from the Sanskrit root *nī* — "to lead, to guide" — the same root that gives *netā*, "leader"; it carries the sense of method, reasoning and sound judgement. **Kai** evokes meeting, connection and shared practice. Read together, the name means clear method, applied in practice: enabling decisions, and building working systems. It is a name with a private family layer, but externally it should read only as a compact, credible technical identity.

## 2. Brand Idea

**Positioning statement:** Naya Kai is a practitioner-led technology identity for data, AI and decision intelligence, combining disciplined thinking with hands-on delivery.

It must never present as:

- A mystical guru or all-seeing AI oracle.
- A cybersecurity, hacking or gamer persona.
- A generic personal-brand consultancy full of buzzwords.
- A corporate monogram without personality.
- A deity, superhero or fantasy-character identity.

It should feel like:

- A senior technical consultant who can both advise and build.
- A calm operator who creates clarity from messy systems.
- An analytical leader who respects evidence, engineering and execution.
- A thoughtful collaborator: serious in substance, lightly distinctive in style.
- Someone who understands that insight only matters when it improves action.

| Archetype | Role in the brand | How it should show up |
|---|---|---|
| **The Pathfinder** | Finds a sound route through ambiguity | Structured problem framing, roadmaps, architectures, decision trees and clear explanations |
| **The Builder** | Converts insight into working systems | Repositories, prototypes, AI workflows, data models, automation and practical documentation |
| **The Guide** | Helps others make confident decisions | Plain language, evidence-led recommendations, reusable templates and generous technical communication |
| **The Craftsperson** | Values clarity, rigour and maintainability | Clean code, simple visual design, thoughtful naming, reliable documentation and attention to detail |
| **The Connector** | Brings data, AI, people and operations together | Collaborative tone, cross-functional examples and a focus on adoption rather than technology for its own sake |

## 3. Narrative

> Naya Kai is built on the idea that better outcomes begin with a better way of seeing and working.
>
> “Naya” represents method, judgement and direction: the discipline of framing the right problem, following the evidence and choosing a path that holds up in practice. “Kai” represents collaboration, craft and shared progress: the work of turning a sound idea into something useful with people, systems and technology.
>
> The result is a technology identity grounded in practical intelligence—using data and AI to reduce complexity, improve decisions and build systems that people can actually use.

## 5. Visual Identity

The central visual metaphor is **a path through a network**: a clear line moves through a field of data points, creating order, direction and connection without trying to dominate the whole system.

The design direction draws on four sources: Japanese restraint, Sanskrit-inspired logic, modern technology, and Australian professional practicality. The result should feel considered and quiet rather than decorative — the cross-cultural roots inform the structure of the mark, not its ornamentation.

| Logo concept | Description | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **The Guided Node** | A minimal circular node with one path entering and two or three clean directional paths emerging | Communicates data flow, judgement and decision pathways without looking generic |
| **The Open Enso Grid** | An incomplete circular stroke intersected by a small structured grid or node pattern | Represents continual learning, clarity and work-in-progress. Use with restraint: the enso must read as an open, unfinished line, not as a Zen symbol |
| **The NK Pathmark** | A bespoke monogram where the “N” becomes a directional path and the “K” becomes a branching junction | Best option for GitHub avatar, favicon and small-format use |
| **The Compass Line** | A restrained compass-like mark made from four data points and a diagonal route | Suggests direction and guidance while avoiding consulting clichés |
| **The Signal Circle** | A clean ring made from connected dots, with one highlighted connection creating a path | Represents collaboration, systems thinking and finding signal within complexity |

### Logo guardrails

- Use a simple geometric mark, not a detailed illustration.
- Ensure it works at 32×32 pixels for repository, platform and IDE avatars.
- Use no gradients in the core logo; gradients can be optional for digital headers only.
- Avoid obvious AI imagery: brains, robots, circuit-board heads, neural-network explosions or chat bubbles.
- Avoid dragons, foxes, phoenixes or mythical creatures as the primary mark.
- Avoid sacred religious iconography or direct use of Sanskrit/Japanese calligraphy unless reviewed by a knowledgeable designer.
- The monogram or path symbol should carry the identity even when the words “Naya Kai” are absent.

### Wordmark hierarchy

| Level | Form | Use |
|---|---|---|

| **Secondary technical lockup** | `naya-kai` | GitHub banner, code project visuals, developer documentation |
| **Compact mark** | `NK` | GitHub avatar, favicon, repository badge, social avatar |

## 6. Colour System

### Colour v2 (proposed)

This is a revised proposal. It supersedes both the earlier v1 palette below and the first v2 draft, narrowing the system to four working roles plus a rare signal colour.

| Role | Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground (dark) | `--nk-ground-dark` | `#141A26` | Banner, dark surfaces, footer |
| Ground (light) | `--nk-ground-light` | `#F5F3EF` | Light surfaces, document background |
| Ink (on light) | `--nk-ink` | `#1B2130` | Body text on light |
| Ink (on dark) | `--nk-ink-inverse` | `#E9EDF3` | Body text on dark |
| Primary | `--nk-indigo` | `#2B3A5C` | Brand anchor, headings, logo |
| Accent | `--nk-teal` | `#1E8C87` | Links, active states, data paths |
| Signal | `--nk-gold` | `#C79A3B` | Rare emphasis only (≤1% of any surface) |

Usage ratio: 60% ground-light, 25% indigo/ink, 10% structural neutrals, 4% teal, 1% gold.

Dark-surface variant: background `#141A26`, surface `#1F2937`, main text `#E9EDF3`, primary indigo `#6F8EDB`, accent teal `#48B7AD`, signal gold `#D8AF5B`, muted text `#AAB4C3`.

## 7. Typography

| Use | Face | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Brand / display | Manrope | Avenir Next, system-ui, sans-serif |
| Body text | Source Sans 3 | system-ui, sans-serif |
| Code / technical | JetBrains Mono | IBM Plex Mono, ui-monospace, monospace |
| Optional long-form | Source Serif 4 | Charter, Georgia, serif |

Avoid calligraphic, brushstroke or faux-Asian display fonts — they make the cross-cultural roots feel decorative rather than authentic.

## 8. Imagery and Graphic Language

### Use

- Fine-line network diagrams and systems maps.
- Minimal directional paths, branching logic and layered grids.
- Abstract signal/noise patterns.
- Clean architecture sketches.
- Subtle topographic lines or flow fields.
- Photography of workspaces, notebooks, whiteboards, city infrastructure, light/shadow and engineering detail.
- Visuals that show process: framing, iteration, flow, connection and reduction of complexity.

### Avoid

- Stock images of people pointing at dashboards.
- Generic glowing AI brains.
- Neon cyberpunk aesthetics.
- Loud hacker imagery, green terminal rain or padlocks.
- Overly ornate Japanese patterns, religious motifs or faux-Sanskrit graphics.
- Fantasy creatures as hero illustrations.
- Future-city visuals that imply hype rather than delivery.

## 9. Taglines

### Strongest

- Enabling decisions. Working systems.
- Sound judgement. Working systems.
- Decisions that hold. Systems that deliver.
- From complexity to useful action.
- Data and AI, made practical.
- Reasoned systems for better decisions.
- Build clarity. Deliver progress.

> **Reading.** The primary tagline is an active promise, not a description of the outputs: Naya Kai *enables decisions* (makes sound, defensible decision-making possible for the client) and *builds working systems* (systems that keep working after the engagement ends). "Working" is the testable adjective — the systems run, and the client can keep using them. If a piece of copy ever needs the verb form spelled out, use "Enabling decisions. Building systems."

### More technical

- Practical systems for data, AI and decisions.
- Data, AI and decision intelligence—built to be used.
- Experiments, systems and tools for clearer decisions.

### More personal

- A considered path through complex systems.
- Method, craft and practical intelligence.
- Finding the useful signal.

### Retired

- ~~Clear method. Useful systems.~~ — superseded by "Enabling decisions. Working systems." The old line under-signalled seniority: no decision layer, no people layer, and "useful" is untestable. Do not use in new material.
